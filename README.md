# Enterprise Event & Awards Marketing: Singapore & Global FinTech Festivals (2024–2026)

**Role:** B2B Marketing Specialist | **Industry:** FinTech / B2B SaaS  
**Key Events & Milestones:** Singapore FinTech Festival (2023,2024, 2025 & 2026), Global FinTech Festival (2024 & 2025), Industry Awards Campaigns  
**Core Stack:** Social Media, HubSpot CRM, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Global & National Newswires

---

## 1. Executive Summary
An end-to-end B2B event and awards marketing framework built to maximize brand authority, booth footfall, executive meeting bookings, and qualified pipeline across flagship international industry gatherings—including the **Singapore FinTech Festival (2023,2024, 2025 & 2026)** and **Global FinTech Festival (2024 & 2025)**—alongside regional FinTech award recognition campaigns.

---

## 2. The Challenge
* **High-Noise Event Environments:** Standing out among hundreds of global FinTech exhibitors required targeted pre-event awareness and account-level engagement before attendees arrived on the floor.
* **Cross-Functional Alignment:** Coordinating launches across product, sales, creative, and leadership teams required strict milestone governance from pre-event promotion through post-event reporting.
* **Multi-Channel Attribution:** Event-driven traffic came from paid social, search, PR syndication, email sequences, and QR booth scans—requiring unified UTM and CRM attribution to measure true pipeline impact.
